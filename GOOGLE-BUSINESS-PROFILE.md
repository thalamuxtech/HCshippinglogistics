# Google Business Profile playbook

**Goal:** rank #1 for shipping searches in Maryland/DC and Lagos, and cover the widest
possible service area around both branches.

Why this matters more than the website work: for "near me" and local-intent searches,
Google shows the **Map Pack** (three business listings with photos, ratings and
distance) *above* the normal blue links. A first-place website result sits below a
third-place Map Pack listing. The site is now technically strong; this is the part
that puts you in that box.

Everything below uses the real business details already in the app, so nothing needs
inventing.

---

## The two profiles

Create **one profile per physical address**. Two separate profiles, not one with a
second address.

| | USA | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Highclass Shipping & Logistics Inc. | Highclass Shipping & Logistics Inc. |
| **Street** | 8611 Westphalia Road | 28 Moleye Street, Alagomeji |
| **City** | Upper Marlboro, Maryland 20774 | Yaba, Lagos |
| **Landmark** | n/a | Behind Sweet Sensation, along Herbert Macaulay Way |
| **Phone** | +1 (240) 374-8394 | +234 808 029 1754 |
| **Website** | https://highclassshippinglogistics.com | https://highclassshippinglogistics.com/contact |
| **Email** | info@highclassshippinglogistics.com | info@highclassshippinglogistics.com |

Start at **business.google.com** → *Add your business*.

> **Use the exact same spelling everywhere.** "Highclass Shipping & Logistics Inc.",
> not "HighClass", not "Highclass Shipping Ltd". Google cross-checks your name, address
> and phone against other sites; mismatches weaken all of them. This is the single most
> common reason a listing underperforms.

### Point the two websites at different pages

Give the Nigeria profile `/contact` rather than the homepage. Two profiles pointing at
the identical URL compete with each other; pointing them at different pages lets each
build its own relevance.

## Categories

Categories decide **which searches you can appear in at all**. Get these wrong and no
amount of reviews will help.

**Primary category (both profiles):** `Freight forwarding service`

**Secondary categories** to add, all that apply:

- Shipping company
- Shipping service
- Courier service
- Logistics service
- Cargo & freight company
- Moving company *(catches "shipping my things to Nigeria" searches)*
- Warehouse *(you have real warehouse space at both ends)*
- Customs broker *(Nigeria profile: you handle clearance)*
- Car shipping service *(RORO)*

The primary carries the most weight. Keep it `Freight forwarding service` on both.

## Service area: how to cover a wide range

This is the part most businesses get wrong.

You have a **storefront customers can visit**, so keep the address **visible** and add a
service area on top. Do *not* hide the address. A hidden address makes you ineligible
for the Map Pack entirely, which is exactly where you want to be.

### USA profile: service areas to add

Add these as service areas (Google allows ~20):

**Maryland:** Upper Marlboro · Bowie · Largo · Landover · Hyattsville · Silver Spring ·
Greenbelt · College Park · Laurel · Bladensburg · Suitland · Clinton · Waldorf ·
Baltimore · Columbia · Rockville · Gaithersburg

**DC & Virginia:** Washington DC · Alexandria · Arlington · Woodbridge

Cap it at roughly a **2-hour drive**. Google demotes listings whose claimed area is
implausibly large for the address, so "the whole East Coast" would hurt you.

### Nigeria profile: service areas to add

**Lagos:** Yaba · Ikeja · Surulere · Mushin · Lagos Island · Victoria Island · Lekki ·
Ikoyi · Apapa · Ajah · Ojota · Maryland · Gbagada · Ketu · Oshodi · Festac · Amuwo-Odofin

**Beyond Lagos:** Ibadan · Abeokuta · Abuja · Port Harcourt

### Covering the other five countries

Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Cameroon and Senegal **cannot** go in the service area: you
have no address there and claiming them risks suspension. Cover them instead through:

- The **business description** (below), which names them.
- The website's `areaServed` schema, which already lists all six countries.
- **Products** (below): one entry per destination country.

## Opening hours

Set these on both profiles. Complete hours are a ranking factor, and a listing with no
hours loses clicks to one that shows "Open now".

**USA:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Sunday closed.

**Nigeria:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Sunday closed.

Adjust to your real hours, but **do not leave them blank**, and do not claim hours you
do not keep. A customer arriving at a closed warehouse writes a one-star review.

Also add:
- **Special hours** for US federal holidays and Nigerian public holidays. Google prompts
  for these and rewards profiles that answer.
- **"More hours"** → set *Pickup* and *Drop-off* windows if they differ from office hours.

## Business description (750 characters)

Paste this into the USA profile:

> Highclass Shipping and Logistics Inc. is an FMC-licensed freight forwarder shipping
> from the USA to Nigeria and across Africa since 2017. We handle sea cargo (boxes,
> barrels, Ghana-must-go bags, drums, furniture and electronics), air freight, and
> vehicle shipping by RORO. Drop off at our Upper Marlboro warehouse or request
> door-to-door pickup anywhere in Maryland, Washington DC and Virginia. Every item is
> weighed, measured, photographed and receipted, then tracked through 8 stages to
> delivery, with digital invoices you can download any time. We also ship to Ghana,
> Kenya, South Africa, Cameroon and Senegal. Registered in Maryland, USA and with the
> CAC in Nigeria.

For the Nigeria profile, swap the middle sentence to:

> Collect from our Yaba office on Herbert Macaulay Way, or we deliver anywhere in Lagos
> and to Ibadan, Abeokuta, Abuja and Port Harcourt. We handle destination customs
> clearance in-house.

Keep the front-loaded keywords ("FMC-licensed freight forwarder", "USA to Nigeria").
Google truncates the description in some views, so the important words must come first.

## Photos: the highest-impact task after reviews

Listings with 10+ photos get **substantially more** calls and direction requests.
Google also ranks fresher photo sets higher, so this is not a one-off job.

### Shoot list (aim for 25-30 per profile)

**Exterior (5 shots)**
Building front with signage · street view showing the entrance · the loading bay ·
parking area · a wide shot that helps someone recognise the place from the road

**Interior (6 shots)**
Reception/counter · the weighing and measuring station · shelving with labelled cargo ·
the barrel and box staging area · a customer being served at the counter · secure storage

**At work (8 shots)**
Staff weighing a barrel · measuring a box · a shipment being photographed and receipted ·
loading a container · a vehicle being driven onto a RORO ramp · a container sealed with
its number visible · handover to a customer · a delivery van at a door

**Team (3 shots)**
Group photo of warehouse staff · a rider with the delivery van · office staff at a desk

**Identity (3 items)**
Logo (square, 720×720 minimum) · cover photo (the strongest exterior or loading shot,
1024×576) · a branded barrel or box

### Photo rules that actually matter

- **Minimum 720×720.** Below that Google may reject or downrank them.
- **Real photos only.** No stock images, no renders, no text overlays or watermarks.
  Google can remove these and repeat offences hurt trust.
- **Enable location on the camera** before shooting. Geotagged photos taken at the
  address are a genuine proximity signal.
- **Upload 3-5 new photos per month**, not 30 at once. Steady freshness outranks a
  single dump.
- Add a short **video** (30 seconds, under 100MB): a barrel arriving, being weighed,
  receipted and loaded. Very few competitors bother, and it stands out in the Map Pack.

## Reviews: the main ranking lever

Volume, recency, rating and **your replies** all count. A profile with 40 recent reviews
beats one with 200 from three years ago.

### Getting them, without breaking the rules

The moment to ask is **when the shipment is marked delivered**. The customer is
relieved and pleased, and it is the peak of goodwill. Send this by WhatsApp:

> Hi {name}, your shipment {tracking} has been delivered. If we did well, a quick
> Google review really helps other families find us: {short link}
> Thank you for shipping with Highclass.

Get the short link from your profile → **Ask for reviews** → *Share review form*.

**Never:**
- Offer a discount or free shipping in exchange for a review. Google removes these and
  can suspend the profile.
- Post reviews yourself or ask staff to. Same-device and same-network reviews are
  detectable.
- Buy reviews. It is the fastest route to suspension.

**Realistic target:** 5-8 genuine reviews per month per profile. At that rate you pass
most local competitors within a year.

### Replying

Reply to **every** review within 48 hours. Google states that responding improves local
ranking, and it is visible to anyone reading.

*Positive:*
> Thank you {name}. Glad the barrels reached Lagos safely. See you on the next sailing.

*Negative (the reply is for the next 100 readers, not the complainant):*
> {name}, I am sorry your shipment was delayed by the vessel schedule. That is outside
> our control, but keeping you informed is not, and we fell short there. I have called
> you to make it right. {your name}, Highclass Shipping

Never argue, never blame the customer, always give a next step. A well-handled
one-star reply often reassures readers more than a five-star review does.

## Products: how you cover destinations and services

Products appear as a browsable panel and let you name things you cannot fit in
categories. Add one per service and per destination.

**By service:**

| Product | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Cargo: small box | From $35 | Boxes, barrels and bags by ocean freight to Nigeria and across Africa. Priced per item, 21-30 business weeks. |
| Sea Cargo: barrel | From $100 | Short and full-size barrels shipped by sea, weighed and receipted before loading. |
| Air Freight | Per lb | Fastest option, roughly 1-2 weeks. Charged on actual or dimensional weight. |
| RORO Vehicle Shipping | Quote | Cars, SUVs and vans by roll-on/roll-off via Grimaldi, Sallaum and MSC. |
| Door-to-door pickup | Quote | We collect from your address in MD, DC or VA. Priced by distance and volume. |

**By destination:** one each for Shipping to Nigeria · Ghana · Kenya · South Africa ·
Cameroon · Senegal. This is how those countries become searchable without putting them
in the service area.

## Attributes to tick

Every attribute you set is another filter you can be found through:

- Identifies as Black-owned *(if applicable: this appears as a badge and drives real traffic)*
- Onsite services · Online appointments
- Wheelchair-accessible entrance and car park *(if true)*
- Free parking on site
- Debit cards, credit cards, mobile payments *(match what you actually take)*
- LGBTQ+ friendly *(optional)*

Only tick what is true. A false attribute invites a report and a review complaint.

## Posts: weekly, 10 minutes

Posts appear in your listing and signal an active business. One a week is enough.

Rotate these four:

1. **Sailing schedule:** "Next container departs Baltimore on the 12th. Drop off by the 10th."
2. **Rate or offer:** "Small boxes to Lagos from $35. Bring your own or buy at the warehouse."
3. **Proof:** a photo of a container being sealed or a delivery handover.
4. **Answer a question:** reuse one from the website FAQ, "How long does shipping to Nigeria take?"

Always finish with a button: *Call now* or *Learn more* → your website.

## The Q&A section

Anyone can post a question on your listing, **including competitors**. Seed it yourself
first with the eight questions already answered on the website FAQ, then upvote your own
answers so they sit at the top.

Copy them from the homepage FAQ so the wording matches what Google already sees on the
site. Consistency between the two reinforces both.

---

## Verification: expect friction

Freight and moving categories are high-fraud, so Google often demands video
verification. Prepare before you start:

1. A **utility bill or lease** showing the business name at the address.
2. Your **FMC licence** document.
3. For the video walkthrough, be ready to show, in one unbroken take: the street sign,
   your signage, the entrance, the interior, and something proving you operate there
   (branded paperwork, the weighing scale, labelled cargo).

If verification fails, do **not** create a second profile, duplicates get both
suspended. Appeal the existing one instead.

---

## First 30 days

**Week 1**, Create both profiles. Start verification. Categories, hours, description,
phone, website.

**Week 2**, Upload the first 15 photos per profile. Add all products. Set service areas
and attributes.

**Week 3**, Seed Q&A from the website FAQ. Publish the first two posts. Start asking
delivered customers for reviews.

**Week 4**, Reply to every review. Add 5 more photos. Publish two more posts. Check
*Performance* to see which search terms are finding you.

**Then, ongoing, about 30 minutes a week:** one post, 3-5 photos a month, reply to every
review within 48 hours, ask every delivered customer.

---

## Also worth doing: consistent citations

Same name, address and phone, character for character, on:

Apple Business Connect · Bing Places · Facebook · Instagram bio · LinkedIn ·
Yelp · Nigerian directories (VConnect, Nairaland business listings) ·
freight directories (FreightNet, ShipmentLink)

Google treats agreement between these as corroboration that you are a real business at
that address. Disagreement is the most common invisible cause of poor local ranking.

---

## What to watch

In **Performance** on each profile, track monthly:

- Searches that found you, are you appearing for "shipping to Nigeria", "barrel
  shipping near me", "freight forwarder Upper Marlboro"?
- Calls, direction requests, website clicks
- Photo views against competitors, Google shows this comparison

**Realistic timeline:** movement in 4-6 weeks, Map Pack for less competitive terms in
2-3 months, a genuine shot at #1 in 4-6 months with steady reviews and photos.

The one thing that will not work is setting the profiles up and leaving them. Ranking
here tracks recent activity, so the weekly half-hour is the actual work.
